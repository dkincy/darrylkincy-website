WHO WE ARE

IT TAKES STRONG MEN MENTORING YOUNG MEN TO MAKE LEADERS.

Taking The Lead Generation is a 501(c)(3) non-profit with the primary mission of providing programs and services to middle and high school boys. We believe no young man should fail in life because he had no one to stand by him as a mentor.

CHARACTER IS KEY

We know from research that just one caring adult in a teen boy’s life has the potential to make a significant impact on his future.

Trusting relationships, non-judgmental conversations, listening “to” and not “through” mentees, and reflection are essential to the learning and growth process for young men.

Our organization delivers on this through introducing strong, compassionate, and caring men of high character who will challenge and inspire the mentees.

HOW THE PROGRAM WORKS

BUILT FOR BOYS — AND THEIR PARENTS

KEYNOTE WELCOME

It’s imperative we have buy-in from not just the boys, but parents as well. We want to be the thermostat — setting the temperature — not the thermometer that just checks it. A welcome keynote for boys and their parents gives a clear picture of who the program is really for: their boys.

WEEKLY “IN-BETWEEN” ZOOM SESSIONS

Live Zoom sessions held once a week give mentees the chance to ask questions and share challenges, struggles, and victories between in-person sessions — designed to include fun learning and stay focused on the mentees, not the mentor-coach.

TEXT & SOCIAL MEDIA

Encouraging text messages and social media provide 24/7 access to training, with Q&A sessions that allow anonymity for parents and boys. Full archive access, with all content subject to review for appropriateness.
